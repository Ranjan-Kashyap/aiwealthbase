## The problem with most AI reviews

Most AI tool content is either an affiliate script or a five-minute surface tour. Neither helps you decide whether a tool belongs in a real income workflow.

## Our testing standard

Every long-form review on AI Wealth Base follows the same shape:

1. **Hook with stakes** — what income or time problem this tool claims to solve
2. **Real workflow** — screen recording on an actual task, not a demo account fantasy
3. **Friction points** — setup pain, pricing traps, quality gaps
4. **Verdict** — who should use it, who shouldn't, cost vs value

## What we refuse to do

- Fake screenshots
- Overnight income claims
- Hiding failed experiments
- Pretending a tool prints money without skill and distribution
